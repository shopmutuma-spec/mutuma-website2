Quick answers for MUTUMA orders and support.

How does checkout work?

Checkout is handled securely through Stripe. Shipping and taxes are confirmed before payment.

How much is shipping?

Europe and US standard shipping is £6. Orders over £50 unlock free shipping automatically.

Can I return an item?

Unused items can be returned within 30 days. Read the returns page for full details.

How do I get order help?

Use the contact page with your order number and checkout email.
