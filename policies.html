Return Policy

Returns within 30 days.

Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery if they are unused, undamaged and in their original packaging.

To start a return, contact MUTUMA with your order email, order number and the item you want to return.

Customers are responsible for return shipping unless the item arrived damaged, faulty or incorrect.

Refund Policy

Refunds after inspection.

Once your return is received and checked, MUTUMA will confirm whether the refund is approved.

Approved refunds are sent back to the original payment method. Your bank or card provider may take extra time to show the refund.

Shipping fees are not refundable unless the order arrived damaged, faulty or incorrect.

Shipping

Tracked delivery.

Europe and US standard shipping is £6. Orders over £50 unlock free shipping automatically at checkout.

Most orders ship in 2-4 business days. Delivery speed depends on destination and carrier handling.

First Order Offer

Email subscribers get a launch code.

First-time visitors can subscribe by email to receive the MUTUMA welcome offer: a discount code plus free shipping on the first item they buy.

Use the code shown after signup at Stripe Checkout. This offer is intended for one use per customer.
